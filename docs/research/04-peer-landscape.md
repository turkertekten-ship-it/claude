# The peer landscape

**Headline.** WAM's peer set has no visible operational AI at all — Turkish managers launched AI as a product theme (Ak Portföy's YZT fund, İstanbul Portföy's Akson GSYF) while zero PYS published any AI in fund operations — and SPK's VII-128.10 requirement that primary and secondary systems sit inside Turkey means the Western vendor stack (Carta, Juniper Square, Affinity) cannot legally be his system of record anyway, making an in-house build the compliance-correct path rather than a budget compromise.

## Findings

### 1. WAM's segment is small and fast-growing: GSYF + GYF together are roughly 669bn TL, about 5% of Turkey's ~14 trillion TL fund market

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://www.ekonomist.com.tr/makale/gsyf-ler-700-milyar-tl-buyukluk-hedefliyor-71192)

GSYF portfolio value was 423.8bn TL on 1 Jan 2026 and 455.7bn TL by 14 April 2026 (SPK data cited by Ekonomist); the GYF sector reached 212.9bn TL in Q1 2026, up 3.5%. Total Turkish investment funds were ~12trn TL at end-2025 and ~14trn TL by April 2026. TKYD put total PYS-managed assets at 11.6trn TL over 11M2025 = 21.4% of GDP. So GSYF+GYF ≈ 668.6bn TL ≈ 4.8% of the fund market. Industry participants expect GSYF alone to hit 700bn TL by end-2026.

### 2. There are 555 GSYFs (499 active) and 298 issuance-approved GYFs — but the average fund is only ~$15-19m and shares a tiny investor pool

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://www.ekonomist.com.tr/makale/gsyf-ler-700-milyar-tl-buyukluk-hedefliyor-71192)

SPK: 555 GSYFs established, 499 active. 477 GSYFs held 17,900 qualified investors at end-2025 — an average of ~38 investors per fund. GYF issuance certificates approved reached 298 as of June 2026. Arithmetic: 455.7bn TL / 499 active GSYF ≈ 913m TL ≈ $19m at 47.2 TRY/USD; 212.9bn TL / 298 GYF ≈ 714m TL ≈ $15m. KAP separately lists ~596 GSYF entries (broader, includes non-active). Minimum aggregate qualified-investor commitment per GSYF was raised from 5m TL to 50m TL.

### 3. Roughly 87 portfolio management companies exist, versus 23 in 2010 — the count is uncertain and no clean 2026 figure is publicly indexed

*Confidence: medium* · [source](https://tspb.org.tr/bilgi/2025-03-donemine-ait-araci-kurum-ve-portfoy-yonetim-sirketleri-verileri-yayimlandi/)

One recent source gives 87 PYS managing 11.2trn TL; another industry account says 'over 80' against 23 in 2010; a mid-2024 practitioner tally counted ~60 PYS issuing funds. SPK's own register page and TSPB's quarterly PYS report are authoritative but were unreachable from this session (spk.gov.tr, tspb.org.tr and kap.org.tr are all egress-blocked here). Treat 80-90 as the working range and verify against the TSPB 2026/Q1 PYS report.

### 4. WAM's closest structural twin is 24 Gayrimenkul ve Girişim Sermayesi Portföy Yönetimi A.Ş. — same licence type, 29 funds

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://24portfoy.com.tr/hakkimizda)

24 Portföy runs 17 GYF and 12 GSYF = 29 funds. Its PRIMO GYF alone was 159.4m TL on 3 Aug 2026. It carries the identical SPK licence category ('gayrimenkul ve girişim sermayesi portföy yönetim şirketi') as WAM. Other same-category boutiques found: Arz Gayrimenkul ve Girişim Sermayesi PYŞ (founded 2015), Akfen Gayrimenkul ve Girişim Sermayesi PYŞ, Alkima Girişim Sermayesi PYŞ, Maxis Girişim Sermayesi PYŞ, Oragon, Pardus, Fonmap, One Portföy, Ata Portföy.

### 5. The scale leaders among independents are Re-Pie ($1.2bn, 62 funds) and Neo Portföy (27 GYF, 15.6bn TL) — and Re-Pie's growth came from pension money

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://www.ekonomim.com/kose-yazisi/12-milyar-dolarin-780-milyon-dolarini-besten-topladi-85-sirkete-yatirdi/701339)

Re-Pie: 62 funds, $1.2bn AUM, 85 portfolio companies, 8.9bn TL of that in GYF, as of 30 June 2026; $780m of the $1.2bn was raised from BES (private pension) funds. Neo Portföy: 27 GYFs totalling 15.6bn TL with 4 more in SPK approval in 2026. Tera Portföy is the largest non-bank PYS overall at >260bn TL (mostly liquid funds), with investors up from ~120k to ~220k during 2026.

### 6. Turkish VC brands are usually NOT the licensed PYS — they rent one. This structural fact defines WAM's competitive position

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://www.unluco.com/basin-odasi/basin-bultenleri/212-ve-unlu-portfoy-den-girisim-sermayesi-yatirim-fonu-hedef-15-milyon-euro/)

212 launched a €15m GSYF through ÜNLÜ Portföy. APY Ventures is a fund umbrella brand of Albaraka Portföy Yönetimi A.Ş., running 8 GSYFs (Fintech, Bilişim Vadisi, Start-up, OSTİM, T3, İvedik, Yeni Nesil Teknoloji, THY), $81.8m fund size, $40m deployed, 73 active startups worth >$1bn combined, and 3 exchange-traded GSYFs (a Turkish first). Boğaziçi Ventures uses Gedik Portföy. Logo Ventures GSYF sits with İstanbul Portföy/Actus. Actera, Turkven, Mediterra and Esas are offshore-GP private equity outside the SPK PYS regime entirely (Actera: >$3.3bn advised, 70,000 portfolio employees, >$5bn revenues).

### 7. DECISIVE CONSTRAINT: SPK requires a capital markets institution's primary AND secondary information systems to be located inside Turkey

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://spl.com.tr/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Bilgi-Sistemleri-Yonetimine-Iliskin-Usul-Ve-Esaslar-Tebligi-VII-128.10.pdf)

Bilgi Sistemleri Yönetimi Tebliği VII-128.9 required institutions to establish business continuity plans with secondary systems, and to keep primary and secondary systems domestically in Turkey, with the secondary site chosen so it does not share natural/environmental disaster risk with the primary. It was replaced by VII-128.10 (Resmî Gazete 32840, published 13 March 2025, in force 30 June 2025), which extends scope to crypto asset service providers and adds a mandatory annual information-systems risk analysis over all information assets with senior-management-approved risk criteria. This makes US-hosted Carta / Juniper Square / Affinity legally awkward as a system of record for a Turkish PYS — not merely a procurement preference.

### 8. An independent information systems audit is mandatory every two years for PYS above the 5m TL minimum-equity threshold

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://spk.gov.tr/data/636df0c51b41c61a944eb99a/III-62.pdf)

Bilgi Sistemleri Bağımsız Denetim Tebliği III-62.2: partially and broadly authorised brokerages and portfolio management companies whose minimum equity obligation exceeds 5m TL must obtain an independent IS audit once every two years. PYS with a minimum equity obligation of 5m TL or less are exempted from specified clauses ('bilgi güvenliği ihlali' and 'değişiklik yönetimi'). Any decision-support system WAM builds becomes in-scope evidence for that audit — logging, versioning, access control and documented change management must be designed in, not retrofitted.

### 9. NON-OBVIOUS ACCOUNTING SPLIT: WAM A.Ş. applies TMS 29 inflation accounting; the funds it manages do not

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://www.erdem-erdem.av.tr/bilgi-bankasi/spknin-ihraccilar-ve-sermaye-piyasasi-kurumlari-icin-verdigi-tms-29-ve-enflasyon-muhasebesi-uygulamasina-iliskin-karari)

SPK moved issuers and capital markets institutions to TMS 29 starting with annual reports for periods ending 31.12.2023. But SPK Karar Organı decision dated 16.02.2024, no. 11/255 ruled that investment funds shall NOT apply inflation accounting in their TMS/TFRS financial statements — covering exchange-traded funds, gayrimenkul yatırım fonları and girişim sermayesi yatırım fonları. Consequence: any consolidated view mixing the management company with its funds mixes restated and nominal TRY. With CPI at 31.75% y/y this is a material, silent error mode.

### 10. The 23 July 2026 valuation decision has a precise reference (Kurul Kararı 45/1359) and turns a market-price lookup into a per-founder NAV ingestion job

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://www.aa.com.tr/tr/ekonomi/spkden-gayrimenkul-ve-girisim-sermayesi-yatirim-fonlarinda-saglikli-fiyatlama-adimi/4008891)

SPK Kurul Kararı dated 23 July 2026, no. 45/1359, communicated to TSPB: exchange-traded GYF and GSYF participation units held in investment fund portfolios must be valued at the last unit value announced by the founder portfolio management company, not the last exchange price. Funds pricing these at exchange prices had until 31 July 2026 to comply. Stated aim: stop unhealthy price formation in exchange-traded GYF/GSYF units; a one-off downward correction was expected in over-priced funds. The valuation input is now published by each founder via KAP, not by Borsa İstanbul — i.e. a daily, per-founder, per-fund ingestion.

### 11. The staffing floor for a GYF+GSYF PYS is set by regulation at roughly 8-12 licensed roles — exactly the boutique shape

*Confidence: medium* · [source](https://ekici-sahin.av.tr/portfoy-yonetim-sirketlerinin-kurulusu-faaliyet-alanlari-ve-duzenleyici-rejimi)

III-55.1 and current SPK practice: minimum initial capital 75m TL, fully paid in cash (a capital commitment is not sufficient); minimum equity tiers of 30m / 40m / 50m / 100m TL for AUM bands up to 1bn, 1-4bn, 4-36bn and over 36bn TL (revalued periodically, so 2026 figures may be higher). Minimum portfolio managers: 3, 4, 5 or 6 by the same AUM bands, all holding SPF Level 3 plus Derivatives licences. Mandatory named roles include genel müdür, iç denetçi, uyum görevlisi and fon operasyon sorumlusu. A GYF-authorised PYS must additionally employ in-house at least one licensed real estate appraisal expert, plus an investment committee of at least three including a board member and the GM.

### 12. Turkish GSYF valuation cadence is far looser than GYF — which is where discretion, and therefore audit risk, concentrates

*Confidence: medium* · [source](https://mevzuat.gov.tr/File/GeneratePdf?mevzuatNo=19220&mevzuatTur=Teblig&mevzuatTertip=5)

III-52.3 (GYF): real estate must be valued at least at each calendar year end by an SPK-approved appraisal firm. III-52.4 (GSYF): the unit value must be calculated and reported to qualified investors at least once a year, and venture investments valued at least as of the accounting period end — no equivalent mandatory independent appraiser cadence. Venture company debt issuances of 5m TL or more require audited or limited-reviewed SPK-format financials; below 5m TL, VUK-basis financials reported by a certified accountant suffice. Companies with 40%+ of assets in real estate, or construction firms, cannot be counted as venture companies.

### 13. THE FIELD IS EMPTY: no Turkish PYS, GSYF or GYF manager was found publishing any operational AI or data-platform use

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://valueaddvc.com/blog/the-vc-tech-stack-in-2026-what-top-funds-are-using-for-deal-flow-crm-and-portfolio)

Searches in Turkish and English across press, vendor sites, association material and job boards surfaced zero cases of a Turkish portfolio management company using AI/ML for fund operations, valuation, LP reporting, portfolio monitoring or deal sourcing. No Turkish PYS data-scientist or data-engineer postings surfaced. No RPA or back-office automation case study in Turkish fund operations (KPMG Türkiye's RPA material with Robusta Cognitive Automation is generic financial services). No Turkish client, office or case study found for eFront, Allvue, Carta, Juniper Square, Vestberry, Bipsync, Affinity, 4Degrees, Chronograph, Colmore or Vessel. This is absence of evidence in indexed public sources, not proof of universal absence — but the gap is wide and consistent across a dozen query formulations.

### 14. What Turkish managers DID do with AI in 2026 is sell it as a product theme, not run it in the back office

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://www.cnbce.com/sirket-haberleri/ak-portfoy-yapay-zeka-fonunu-baslatti-sektorde-bir-ilk-h31909)

Ak Portföy launched Turkey's first and only AI Technologies Companies fund on TEFAS (code YZT) plus a Robotics fund (DTZ); GM Mehmet Ali Ersarı framed it around AI-driven economic value and expected OpenAI/Anthropic IPOs in H2 2026. İstanbul Portföy runs 'Akson Yapay Zeka Teknolojileri GSYF'. BV Portföy runs an AI-algorithm statistical arbitrage hedge fund (İstatistiksel Arbitraj Serbest TL). Corporate GSYFs targeting AI startups: Turkcell GSYF, THY GSYF (first investment Vendorside, May 2026), İş Bankası's Yapay Zeka Fabrikası. Every one is AI as an asset class or a trading signal — none is AI in fund administration.

### 15. The gap is quantified: Turkey's AI maturity is 2.30 and asset management is not even a broken-out sector, against 55% of global asset managers already running AI in an investment process

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://digitopia.co/impact-labs/reports/turkiye-dijital-ve-yapay-zeka-olgunluk-raporu-2026/)

Digitopia Türkiye Dijital ve Yapay Zeka Olgunluk Raporu 2026 (global: 46 countries, 44 sectors and sub-sectors, 1,575 measurements, 33,870 participants, 103,500 structured data points; Turkey leg: 551 measurements, 11,847 participants, 36,201 data points) — Turkey's AI maturity rose 2.10 to 2.30 and digital maturity 2.87 to 2.97, explicitly described as limited relative to the market's AI leap. Banking scores 3.26, above the national average; automotive 3.16, airports 3.05. Asset/portfolio management does not appear as a reported sector. Contrast: Mercer's 2026 asset-manager survey found 55% have AI integrated in at least one investment process, 27% at pilot/PoC, 18% none.

### 16. The reachable vendor landscape is domestic and thin: İnfina (PM1/İnfleks) is the incumbent, Solvera is the only GSYF-specific product found

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://infina.com.tr/pm1-portfoy-yonetimi.html)

İnfina Yazılım A.Ş., founded 1995, FinBase from 1996; since 2004 a strategic partnership with Swiss Expersoft AG whose PM1 portfolio management/BI product is deployed at leading Turkish PYS; the İnfleks ecosystem spans front, middle and back office for banks, brokers, PYS, funds, custodians, pension and insurance firms. Fonet Bilgi Teknolojileri supplies market data, trading platforms and order/risk management; Matriks supplies market data. Solvera Yazılım markets a purpose-built 'GSYF Yönetim Sistemi' covering application intake, announcement/event tracking, investment evaluation, reporting and operational process management with mail-integrated notifications — no public pricing or client list. Tellingly it is İnfina, the vendor, publishing on AI (TKYD Kurumsal Yatırımcı, April 2026) rather than any manager.

### 17. A full Western VC/PE software stack costs $60-120k a year — about 2.8-5.7m TRY at 47.2 — before data residency makes it unusable as a system of record

*Confidence: medium* · [source](https://valueaddvc.com/blog/the-vc-tech-stack-in-2026-what-top-funds-are-using-for-deal-flow-crm-and-portfolio)

2026 US market anchors: Affinity or 4Degrees relationship CRM ~$15-25k/yr (4Degrees ~$500-800 per user per month for small teams); Harmonic or PitchBook sourcing $12-36k/yr; Visible or Juniper Square portfolio monitoring $5-20k/yr; total annual software spend for a $50-100m fund $60-120k. Juniper Square historically ~$18k/yr, now quote-only; Carta also quote-based. Sub-$50M funds are widely reported still running on spreadsheets, Notion or Airtable, which holds until inbound deal flow passes roughly 50-100 opportunities per quarter — a threshold WAM's Pitch Challenge volume already exceeds.

### 18. Free Turkish institutional data rails already exist and are the realistic substrate for a Motherbrain-style system — including GEFAS, purpose-built for GYF

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://gefas.gov.tr/gyf)

GEFAS (Gayrimenkule Dayalı ve Gelişen Finansal Araçlar Bilgi Sistemi, gefas.gov.tr), operated by MKK, publishes product, issuer and performance data for exchange-traded gayrimenkul yatırım fonu, GYO shares, gayrimenkul sertifikası and kira sertifikası — 1/3/6-month and 1-year returns plus weekly to 5-year price and volume series in both TRY and USD, with an English interface and per-fund detail pages (e.g. RPFGY.F1, ALDUK.F1, APYMD.F2). Alongside it: KAP (fund disclosures, founder-announced unit values, portfolio reports), TEFAS (Takasbank's platform, live since January 2015, fully automated settlement/custody with MKK), SPK bulletins and portfolio value tables, FonVeri (fonveri.com/kurucular) and Fintables (mirrors KAP attachments). Custody itself is outsourced to one of ~13 authorised portföy saklayıcısı including İş Bankası, Garanti, Denizbank, Halkbank, Ziraat, TEB, Ak Yatırım, Finans Yatırım and Takasbank.

## Implications for a small Istanbul GSYF/GYF manager

- Build, don't buy — for a regulatory reason, not a budget one. SPK VII-128.10 (in force 30 June 2025) carries forward the requirement that primary AND secondary information systems be located inside Turkey. That disqualifies US-hosted Carta / Juniper Square / Affinity as the system of record for a Turkish PYS. The MIT finding that external vendor tools succeed twice as often as internal builds does not transfer here, because the vendors are not legally deployable as systems of record. oodarag on Turkish infrastructure is the architecturally correct answer, and a $60-120k/yr Western stack (2.8-5.7m TRY at 47.2) would buy him a compliance problem.
- Design for the auditor from line one. III-62.2 requires an independent information systems audit every two years for a PYS above the 5m TL minimum-equity threshold, and VII-128.10 requires an annual, senior-management-approved risk analysis covering ALL information assets. Anything he builds is in scope. Immutable logs, versioned prompts and models, role-based access, documented change management and written data lineage from source to reported number — retrofitting these after the first IS audit finding costs far more than building them in, as he knows from the controllership side.
- Put a restated/not-restated flag on every TRY figure in the system. SPK decision 16.02.2024 no. 11/255 exempts investment funds — including GYF and GSYF — from TMS 29, while WAM A.Ş. itself applies inflation accounting from FY2023. A consolidated management view without that flag silently adds inflation-adjusted company numbers to nominal fund numbers. At 31.75% CPI this is not a rounding error; it is the single highest-value correctness invariant in the whole design.
- Make SPK Kurul Kararı 45/1359 (23 July 2026) the first production data job. For every exchange-traded GYF/GSYF unit anywhere in a portfolio, the valuation input is now the founder's last announced unit value, published per founder via KAP rather than by Borsa İstanbul. That is a daily, deterministic, per-founder ingestion with a single right answer — exactly where an agent connected to real institutional data beats a chatbot, and exactly where a wrong number becomes a compliance event.
- His Motherbrain substrate already exists and is free: GEFAS (MKK) for GYF/GYO product, issuer and performance series in TRY and USD across 1w-5y; KAP for founder unit values, fund general information and portfolio reports; TEFAS; SPK bulletins and portfolio value tables; FonVeri for founder-level fund listings. The EQT lesson was capture everything behind an investment and connect it to real institutional data — in Turkey that means an ingestion layer over these five rails, not a vendor subscription.
- Spend the AI budget on document-heavy work and keep it out of the numerical core. GSYF/GYF operations are dominated by içtüzük and ihraç belgesi, SPK-licensed appraisal reports, KAP filings, SPK bulletins, audit files, side letters and portfolio-company financials — the one area where global PE evidence (diligence highest at 31% integration), fund-administration consensus and his own operating reality all agree. Deterministic code must own NAV, unit value, waterfall and fee accrual, with AI as an extraction layer over it, never inside it.
- Do not build deal sourcing or portfolio-monitoring AI (64% and 75% of PE respondents rate them ineffective), and note that no Turkish dataset is dense enough to beat what he already owns. The Pitch Challenge series — 498 pitches, 395 unique startups, 9 events across Istanbul, Ankara and Izmir since March 2022 — IS proprietary sourcing data. Digitising and linking that corpus (who pitched, who attended, who introduced whom, what happened since) is worth more than any Affinity or Harmonic licence and is not blocked by data residency.
- The LP pool is brutally concentrated, so relationship-data quality beats volume: 477 GSYFs shared just 17,900 qualified investors at end-2025, roughly 38 per fund, with a 50m TL minimum aggregate commitment per fund. His holding-company pipeline (Yıldız, Anadolu, Tekfen, Arçelik, Eko) chases the same handful of names as every peer. Separately, pension capital is the proven growth engine — Re-Pie raised $780m of its $1.2bn from BES funds — and a BES LP expects an institutional reporting cadence, which is a systems capability to build before the raise, not after.
- Optimise per-fund marginal cost toward zero, not intelligence. The visible growth path here is fund count: 24 Portföy at 29 funds, Neo at 27 GYF, Re-Pie at 62. A 2022-vintage PYS reaches that scale by making fund number 15 cost almost nothing to onboard, price, value, audit and report. That, not model sophistication, is the design objective every decision should be judged against.
- The competitive bar is Excel plus İnfina PM1, and only one competitor is worth watching closely. No peer — bank-affiliated or independent — has published any operational AI; the one party talking about it is İnfina, the vendor, in TKYD's April 2026 journal. Meanwhile SPK licensed Fintables Portföy Yönetimi A.Ş. in 2025 (60m TL initial capital, 300m TL registered ceiling) — the only data-native firm entering the Turkish PYS market, and therefore the most informative benchmark for, or partner to, what he is building.
