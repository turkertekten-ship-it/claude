# The regulatory and accounting obligation surface

> Delegated research, recorded verbatim. This is a subagent's report:
> second-hand under the doctrine in CLAUDE.md, and not promoted to
> established fact anywhere in this repository. The agent reported that
> page fetching was blocked in its environment, so its findings rest on
> search summaries. Confidence grades are the agent's own. Verify anything
> load-bearing against the primary source before acting on it.

**Headline.** The 23 July 2026 SPK decision (45/1359) turns a fund founder's own announced unit value into the market-wide valuation input for exchange-traded GYF/GSYF units — while SPK separately bars funds from applying TMS 29 — so the NAV WAM publishes is an unindexed nominal-TRY number that other institutions must now book at face in a 31.75% inflation environment.

## Findings

### 1. The tebliğ numbering in the brief is partly wrong; correcting it matters because every citation downstream depends on it

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=19227&MevzuatTur=9&MevzuatTertip=5)

III-52.1 = Yatırım Fonlarına İlişkin Esaslar Tebliği (securities funds). III-52.3 = Gayrimenkul Yatırım Fonlarına İlişkin Esaslar Tebliği (GYF). III-52.4 = Girişim Sermayesi Yatırım Fonlarına İlişkin Esaslar Tebliği (GSYF) — NOT III-52.3. III-55.1 = Portföy Yönetim Şirketleri ve Bu Şirketlerin Faaliyetlerine İlişkin Esaslar Tebliği (the PYŞ tebliği, RG 02/07/2013 no. 28695). III-56.1 = Portföy Saklama Hizmetine İlişkin Esaslar Tebliği (custody). Fund financial reporting sits in II-14.2, not II-14.1; II-14.1 governs the management company itself. Live amendment chains: III-52.3.f (RG 17/07/2024 no. 32604), III-52.4.c (RG 21/09/2024 no. 32669), II-14.2.ç (24/01/2025), III-55.1.d (18/02/2023).

### 2. SPK decision 23/07/2026 no. 45/1359 replaces exchange price with founder-announced NAV for exchange-traded GYF/GSYF participation units held in fund portfolios

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://www.aa.com.tr/tr/ekonomi/spkden-gayrimenkul-ve-girisim-sermayesi-yatirim-fonlarinda-saglikli-fiyatlama-adimi/4008891)

Investment funds holding exchange-traded GYF/GSYF units must value them at the last unit value announced by the founding portfolio management company (net asset value), not the last exchange price. Stated rationale: thin trading volumes produced price formation that did not reflect true fund value. Portfolio management companies had to be compliant by 31 July 2026. Two-sided effect for WAM: as a holder it must re-source valuation inputs; as a founder of exchange-traded funds its announced unit value becomes an externally relied-upon price, not just an investor report.

### 3. Qualified investor / opt-up professional client financial thresholds were raised tenfold in December 2025

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://spk.gov.tr/data/694586788f95db1cf852440a/2025-64.pdf)

SPK Karar Organı decision of 18/12/2025 no. 65/2354, principle decision i-SPK.37.7: the financial assets test for demand-based (talebe dayalı) professional client status rose from TRY 1,000,000 to TRY 10,000,000, and the trading volume test from TRY 500,000 to TRY 5,000,000. Added to the Yatırım Hizmet ve Faaliyetleri ile Yatırım Kuruluşlarına İlişkin Rehber as section BB. Critically, status acquired before the decision's publication is grandfathered and continues. Since GSYF/GYF units may only be sold to nitelikli yatırımcı, this directly narrows the addressable LP base for new investors.

### 4. Investment funds are expressly exempted from TMS 29 inflation accounting; the management company is not

*Confidence: medium* · [source](https://www.erdem-erdem.av.tr/bilgi-bankasi/spknin-ihraccilar-ve-sermaye-piyasasi-kurumlari-icin-verdigi-tms-29-ve-enflasyon-muhasebesi-uygulamasina-iliskin-karari)

SPK Karar Organı decision 16/02/2024 no. 11/255 (reinforced by 07/03/2024 no. 14/382, following 28/12/2023 no. 81/1820) states that investment funds will not apply inflation accounting in financial statements prepared under TMS/TFRS; it was inserted into the Yatırım Fonlarına İlişkin Rehber under Sorumlu Yönetim İlkeleri art. 13. Issuers and sermaye piyasası kurumları — which includes a portföy yönetim şirketi — did move to TMS 29 from the period ending 31/12/2023. I found no decision reversing the fund exemption for 2025 or 2026, but I could not confirm it positively for 2026 from a primary source.

### 5. Tax-side inflation adjustment is suspended for 2025, 2026 and 2027 regardless of whether the statutory thresholds are met

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://tr.andersen.com/tr/images/pdf/and_54-enflasyon-duzeltmesi-uygulanmayacaktir.pdf)

VUK Geçici Madde 37: financial statements are not subject to enflasyon düzeltmesi in the 2026 accounting period (advance tax periods included) whether or not the mükerrer 298 conditions are satisfied. Only taxpayers exclusively dealing in worked gold/silver continue to adjust. The President may extend the suspension by up to three further accounting periods. Separately, for 2024 and 2025 the profit/loss arising from inflation adjustment was excluded from taxable income for banks, 6361 companies, payment/e-money institutions, asset management companies, sermaye piyasası kurumları, insurance and pension companies — so a PYŞ was already ring-fenced. Net effect for 2026: TMS 29 restatement in SPK financials, no inflation adjustment in the tax base — a permanent book/tax divergence to carry.

### 6. Fund financial reporting deadlines under II-14.2 as amended by II-14.2.ç (24/01/2025)

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://www.alomaliye.com/2025/01/24/yatirim-fonlarinin-finansal-raporlama-esaslarina-iliskin-teblig-ii-14-2-c/)

Annual financial reports: filed with SPK within 60 days of accounting-period end; for GYF and GSYF publicly disclosed together with the independent audit report within 90 days of period end (extended from 60). Six-month interim reports: filed with SPK within 30 days of period end; publicly disclosed within 45 days with the review report where review applies. KAP disclosure must occur by close of business on the 6th business day following the date the founder's board resolves to publish, counted from the auditor's signed transmittal reaching the founder. Annual reports are subject to full independent audit; six-month interims to limited review.

### 7. Unit-value announcement frequency is a compliance-cost switch, and qualified-investor-only funds got a reporting exemption

*Confidence: medium* · [source](https://spk.gov.tr/kurumlar/fonlar/yatirim-fonlari/girisim-sermayesi-yatirim-fonlari/tanitim-rehberi)

The baseline is that fund unit value is calculated and notified to qualified investors at least once per accounting period. But GYF and GSYF that announce a unit price more than once within an accounting period have their six-month interim financial statements pulled into limited independent review. Separately, II-14.2.ç exempted funds selling units only to qualified investors (other than serbest fonlar traded on TEFAS) from preparing the portföy dağılım raporu. Some fund issuance documents commit to announcing the monthly purchase price by the 10th business day after each month end — that appears to be a fund-level undertaking in the ihraç belgesi rather than a tebliğ rule, and I could not confirm it as a general obligation.

### 8. The management company files capital adequacy monthly on a five-business-day clock, against tiered minimum equity

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2013/07/20130702-12.htm)

III-55.1: capital adequacy tables plus the number and size of portfolios managed under sub-portfolio management are sent to SPK monthly, within five business days of the end of the relevant period, by SPK-approved means; SPK may change the computation and filing timing. Minimum equity scales with AUM: up to TRY 100m → TRY 2m equity; TRY 100,000,001–500m → TRY 3m; TRY 500,000,001–5bn → TRY 5m; above TRY 5bn → TRY 10m. Minimum founding capital for a PYŞ is TRY 6,000,000. Other crisp clocks: loss of any required condition notified within 3 business days; a departed fon müdürü replaced and notified within 6 business days; executed portfolio management contracts filed within 6 business days of signature.

### 9. Funds owe SPK a quarterly fee of 0.005% of fund total value, payable within 10 business days

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://spk.gov.tr/data/61e4a3a01b41c60d1404d7d2/Yat%C4%B1r%C4%B1m%20Fonlar%C4%B1na%20%C4%B0li%C5%9Fkin%20Rehber.pdf)

Under Capital Markets Law 6362 art. 130(3), on the last business day of each three-month period on a calendar-year basis, the founder calculates a fee of yüz binde beş (five per hundred thousand = 0.005%) of the fund's total value; the portfolio custodian approves it; the founder pays it into the SPK account within the following ten business days. The charge is expensed in the fund's records and met from fund assets. This is one of the few genuinely mechanical, cash-moving quarterly items in the whole surface.

### 10. GSYF venture valuation: annual internal valuation, with independent valuation-firm reports on a size-tiered frequency raised sharply in September 2024

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://www.alomaliye.com/2024/09/21/girisim-sermayesi-yatirim-fonlarina-iliskin-esaslar-tebligi-iii-52-4-c/)

III-52.4 requires venture capital investments to be valued at minimum as at each calendar year end by SPK-approved valuation firms. III-52.4.c (RG 21/09/2024 no. 32669) reset the size bands governing how often a valuation-firm report is required: below TRY 25m → every third accounting period; TRY 25–50m → every second accounting period; above TRY 50m → every accounting period. The prior bands were TRY 5m and TRY 5–10m, so the thresholds rose fivefold, materially reducing report frequency for smaller positions. A valuation-firm report dated within the three months before period end may be adopted as the year-end value by investment committee resolution.

### 11. GYF appraisal has a hard 31 December completion date and two short internal handoff clocks

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=19227&MevzuatTur=9&MevzuatTertip=5)

III-52.3: rayiç değer and rayiç kira bedeli of assets and rights entering the portfolio must be determined by SPK-listed gayrimenkul değerleme kuruluşları, and purchases, sales and leases must be executed by reference to those determined values. Any portfolio asset whose rayiç değer was not determined for any reason during the last three months of the accounting period must have a year-end valuation performed. The appraisal work must be completed no later than the last day of the relevant year; no more than five business days may elapse between the end of the valuation fieldwork and the report date; and the report must be delivered to the founder within two business days of the report date.

### 12. GYF corporate tax exemption is now conditional on distributing at least 50% of immovable-derived income, clarified by a May 2026 communiqué

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2026/05/20260524-3.htm)

Law 7524 made the KVK 5/1-d exemption for gayrimenkul yatırım fonları and ortaklıkları conditional, for income earned from 1/1/2025, on distributing at least 50% of income derived from immovables as dividend by the end of the second month following the month in which the corporate tax return is due — i.e. by 30 June for calendar-year entities. If not distributed in that window, the tax that would have accrued is treated as lost revenue (vergi ziyaı). Kurumlar Vergisi Genel Tebliği Seri No. 25 (RG 24/05/2026 no. 33263) added clarifications, including that advance dividends (avans kâr payı) count toward the test, that common general expenses are allocated proportionately in computing the immovable income base, and that funds which cannot hold immovables under their scope need not apply the condition.

### 13. GSYF is treated more favourably than GYF: no distribution condition, and minimum corporate tax reaches only immovable income

*Confidence: medium* · [source](https://www.pwc.com.tr/tr/sektorler/gayrimenkul/bultenler/2024/1-ocak-2025ten-sonra-gyo-ve-gyfler-icin-vergide-ne-degisiyor.html)

The 50% distribution condition attaches to immovable-derived income of GYF/GYO; a pure venture fund with no immovables has no such income and therefore no condition to satisfy. For yurt içi asgari kurumlar vergisi (KVK 32/C), only income that funds and partnerships derive from immovables enters the minimum tax base — and it enters whether or not distributed — so GSYF venture income sits outside it. Corporate holders' dividends from full-liability GSYF units and GSYO shares remain within the KVK 5/1-a participation exemption; funds whose portfolios hold FX-denominated assets, gold or other precious metals and instruments based on them are excluded from that exemption.

### 14. Withholding on GSYF/GYF units was re-set in March 2026: 0% above a two-year holding period, 17.5% otherwise

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://www.isportfoy.com.tr/medya-ve-blog/yatirim-fonlarina-uygulanan-stopaj-duzenlemesi-hakkinda-mart-2026)

Presidential Decree no. 11107 of 26/03/2026, published in Resmî Gazete of 27/03/2026 no. 33206, re-regulated withholding on income from investment fund participation units. GSYF and GYF units held more than two years remain at 0%; other investment funds are at 17.5%. The change notably brought equity-intensive serbest fon units not traded on TEFAS into the 17.5% band. The two-year holding test is therefore a per-investor, per-lot state variable that determines the tax outcome at redemption.

### 15. A new YMM certification report requirement attaches to corporate tax exemptions from the 2025 accounting year

*Confidence: medium* · [source](https://vergimerkezi.com.tr/ymm-calisma-zorunlulugu-istisna-indirim-2026/)

YMM Genel Tebliği Seri No. 49, published in Resmî Gazete on 30/12/2025, made a YMM tasdik raporu mandatory for taxpayers claiming exemptions and deductions above threshold. Reported triggers: 'zarar olsa dahi indirilecek istisna ve indirimler' total exceeding TRY 1,000,000; amounts entered under 'diğer indirimler' or 'diğer istisnalar' exceeding TRY 1,000,000; or aggregate exemptions and deductions reaching TRY 1,000,000 even where no single line exceeds TRY 500,000. No amount threshold applies to reduced-rate benefit under KVK 32/A. This is a new annual professional-fee and evidence-assembly obligation attaching to the very exemptions WAM and its funds rely on. Thresholds are as reported by secondary commentary; I could not verify them against the tebliğ text.

### 16. MASAK obligations run on a ten-business-day suspicious transaction clock with an eight-year record retention tail

*Confidence: high* · [source](https://masak.hmb.gov.tr/suc-gelirlerinin-aklanmasinin-ve-terorun-finansmaninin-onlenmesine-iliskin-yukumluluklere-uyum-programi-hakkinda-yonetmelik/)

A portföy yönetim şirketi is a sermaye piyasası kurumu and therefore a yükümlü under Law 5549. Tedbirler Yönetmeliği art. 28/2: suspicious transactions must be reported to MASAK within at most ten business days of the suspicion arising, and immediately where delay would be prejudicial. Reporting is performed by the appointed uyum görevlisi where one exists. Law 5549 art. 8 retention: documents for eight years from their date, books and records from the last entry date, identity-verification records from the last transaction date. The Uyum Programı Yönetmeliği additionally requires a compliance programme with a compliance officer, institutional risk policy, monitoring and controls, training and internal audit.

### 17. Internal control and IT audit obligations are scaled, and a small PYŞ may lawfully outsource most control functions

*Confidence: medium* · [source](https://spk.gov.tr/data/636df0c51b41c61a944eb99a/III-62.pdf)

III-55.1 requires a PYŞ to establish an internal control and risk management system, an inspection (teftiş) unit and a fund service unit, with written job descriptions requiring staff to escalate practices inconsistent with policy or law. Article 19 permits obtaining inspection, internal control, research, fund service unit, risk management and information systems services from outside — from investment institutions or from companies with those units — subject to SPK permission and board oversight, with the permitted scope keyed to the managed-portfolio size tiers in art. 28/1. In smaller tiers a müfettiş may discharge the internal control officer's duties if experience conditions are met. Under III-62.2, information systems independent audit is required every two years where equity exceeds TRY 5m and every three years where equity is TRY 5m or less; VII-128.9 imposes the underlying IT governance and internal control framework.

## Implications for a small Istanbul GSYF/GYF manager

- Model the obligations calendar as a dependency graph, not a date list. The binding constraint is almost never the filing date — it is the upstream input. The 90-day fund annual disclosure is downstream of a GYF appraisal that must be finished by 31 December and delivered within 2 business days of report date, or of a GSYF valuation-firm report whose very existence depends on a size tier. Each terminal deadline needs its predecessors modelled with their own lead times and owners.
- Treat the announced unit value as the highest-consequence number WAM produces. Since 31 July 2026 other institutions must book WAM's exchange-traded units at WAM's last announced NAV. That argues for a NAV register with immutable timestamps, the approving resolution, and a pointer to the underlying appraisal or valuation report — an audit trail built for external reliance, not internal reporting.
- Encode announcement frequency as a policy switch with a modelled cost. Announcing a unit price more than once per accounting period pulls the six-month interim financial statements into limited independent review. That is a real fee and calendar consequence that should be visible when the frequency decision is made, not discovered afterwards.
- Carry two return series, always. Funds do not apply TMS 29, so every reported fund return is nominal TRY against 31.75% CPI. A decision-support system should compute a TÜFE-deflated real view beside the regulatory nominal NAV and never let the two be confused in an LP-facing view — precisely the distinction his HCP tracking habit already trains for.
- Add an eligibility field with a grandfathering date to the LP pipeline tracker. The opt-up threshold moved from TRY 1m to TRY 10m on 18/12/2025, but status acquired before that date persists. Whether a target contact is already a nitelikli yatırımcı, and since when, is now a first-class qualification variable for the Yıldız / Anadolu / Tekfen / Arçelik / Eko pipeline.
- Build the schema around predicates, not fixed cadences. The genuinely dangerous obligations are conditional: GSYF valuation frequency keyed to TRY 25m/50m per investment; IT audit cadence keyed to TRY 5m equity; YMM report keyed to a TRY 1m exemption total; the 50% distribution test keyed to whether a fund holds immovables at all. A flat calendar silently misses all of these. Each obligation object needs an applicability predicate over fund and company attributes.
- Keep the numeric core deterministic. NAV, portfolio limits, capital adequacy, the 0.005% quarterly SPK fee and the two-year withholding holding test must be computed and reconciled in code, not generated by a model. This matches the fund-administration evidence already gathered: AI is a supporting document layer over a deterministic numerical core, and 66% of fund accountants' pain is integration, not intelligence.
- Point AI at the document layer, where the evidence says it works. Extracting fields from appraisal and valuation reports, ihraç belgesi and investor agreement terms, and YMM report inputs is high-volume, verifiable, document-heavy work — the category with the highest measured traction. This is also the input side of the dependency graph above, so it compounds.
- Make regulatory change-watch a product feature, not a one-off rulebook. At least six material changes landed in fourteen months: III-52.4.c (Sep 2024), II-14.2.ç (Jan 2025), i-SPK.37.7 (Dec 2025), YMM Tebliği 49 (Dec 2025), Presidential Decree 11107 (Mar 2026), KVGT 25 (May 2026), SPK 45/1359 (Jul 2026) — plus a pending Rehber revision. A monitored feed over the SPK bülten, Resmî Gazete and KAP will age better than any static obligation table.
- Verify before hard-coding. No primary source was reachable from this environment; every article number and business-day count here is second-hand. Before this seeds production code, have counsel or the compliance officer confirm the tebliğ text — particularly every 'iş günü' versus 'gün' distinction, the II-14.2 60/90-day split, and the YMM Tebliği 49 thresholds.
